---
title: Have You Ever
date: 2026-04-22
---

have you ever sat into your self?</br>
felt deltoids retract into spine,</br>
felt worry melt into fine</br>
strands of heat, shivering up</br>
the neck's incline</br>
and helmeting your face?</br>
</br>
have you ever held your space?</br>
the circle that's inscribed</br>
on the meters that surround</br>
your body? have you ever reached</br>
into that sphere</br>
and recovered distance</br>
jotted down time</br>
analyzed velocity</br>
and finned through air?</br>
</br>
ever thought of yourself as a shark</br>
or a knife, or a brush</br>
ever conceptualized your nerves as thousands</br>
of sensors, coiled in brine,</br>
ever considered you're like a tree</br>
blowing in perennial breezes</br>
prevailing winds of spatial configuration?</br>
</br>
have you ever reached out to another in anger</br>
and found only reciprocity</br>
only likeness, like a mirror but warmer</br>
like mercury but steady</br>
ever felt so alive with another that it felt like</br>
your brain might burst from a chthonic slumber</br>
from a rotten iceberg</br>
into lightning-laced air?</br>
</br>
because I have</br>
and I've never recovered</br>
